Why does Google withhold these queries? Under Google Search Console's official Privacy Threshold, Google does not display the exact search terms when a query is made by very few people or contains personal, sensitive, or precise GPS location details (e.g. "breakfast near 238 inman ave open now", "estimes cafe colonia phone call", or "haitian catering near me today"). Google protects user privacy by withholding the text in the query table, but credits 100% of these 245 clicks and 3,875 impressions to your site total, confirming they are genuine local diners arriving at your menu and ordering pages.
