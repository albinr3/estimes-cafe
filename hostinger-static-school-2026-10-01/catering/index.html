Party Trays & Catering FAQs

How many people do your party trays serve?

Half trays typically serve 8–10 guests, and full trays typically serve 15–20 guests. Tell us your guest count and we will help you build the right order.

Do you offer office lunch catering in Woodbridge and Central NJ?

Yes. Estime's Café prepares office lunch catering and party trays for delivery across Woodbridge Township, Colonia, Edison, and surrounding Central NJ communities.

Can I mix different trays for a corporate lunch or celebration?

Yes. Combine pastas, proteins, seafood, salads, breakfast favorites, and brunch selections to create a menu that fits your guests and budget.
